Akan Name Generator

What This App Does

Discover your Akan name! Akan names are traditional Ghanaian names given based on the day of the week you were born and your gender.

  • Enter Your Birthday: Use the simple calendar to pick your date of birth.
  • Select Your Gender: Choose male or female from a dropdown.
  • Get Your Name: The app will instantly calculate the day of the week you were born and show you your unique Akan name!

Whether you’re curious about your cultural roots or just want to learn something new, this app makes it quick, fun, and easy.
